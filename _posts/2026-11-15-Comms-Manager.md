aaDH is seeking a Communications Manager to join our Executive Committee! 

![Image listing key responsibilities of the role: 1. 1-year term (renewable at 2027 aaDH AGM; 2. Time commitment: approx 1 x hour per week + 1-hour committee meeting every two months; 3. Duties: maintain social media engagement, website updates etc. and work closely with aaDH President to promote workshops, events & other aaDH initiatives; 4. The role is ideal for a PhD/MA candidate or ECR who wants to develop their committee/research engagement experience and DH networks, in Australasia and globally; 5. NB: this is an academic service role, not a paid position.)](/assets/images/2026-comms-manager-ad.jpg)

The role will work closely with the aaDH President to develop ideas for DH workshops, events, and promotion of the Association's activities. 
The Communications Manager will also work alongside the President to manage aaDH social media activities. 
Time commitment: approx. 1 hour per week + 1 hour committee meeting every 2-3 months.

To apply: send a 150 word EOI about why you would like to do this role + a CV to Tyne Sumner by 25th September 
tyne.sumner@anu.edu.au
